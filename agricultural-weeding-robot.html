02 / My contribution

Mechanical design for a field robotics challenge

The project targets weed control across agricultural crop beds. As co-founder and lead mechanical design engineer, I worked from toolhead architecture through fabrication and hardware integration.

01

Toolhead architecture

2-DOF pan and tilt

I designed and fabricated weeding toolheads with V-shaped catching mechanisms for reach and precise actuation across different crop beds.

02

Quick-change hardware

Implement swaps under 2 minutes

I designed a rack-rail clamp assembly for fast changes, with positional accuracy and sealing against dust and moisture.

03

System integration

Mechanical and electronic hardware

I worked with the engineering team to integrate BLDC motors, drivers, custom enclosures and encoder feedback, with CAN-bus and ROS 2 control.

04

Design to fabrication

Workshop-ready details

I coordinated supplier and in-house fabrication and prepared technical specifications, GD&T drawings and tolerance requirements for technicians.
